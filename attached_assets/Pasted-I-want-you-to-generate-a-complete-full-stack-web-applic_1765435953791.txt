Notification triggers (message notifications, found-item updates)

🔥 PROJECT GOAL

A system where:

A Passenger loses an item while traveling.

They select transport mode → route/location → enter vehicle details → describe lost item.

The Driver receives the request in their dashboard.

If the driver finds something, they upload photos + description.

Passenger gets notified.

Passenger can view driver contact details (after item is marked found).

Passenger and Driver can chat in real time via Conversations Center.

Both can arrange handover of item.
